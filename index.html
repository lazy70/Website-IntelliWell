WHO WE ARE

Built at the intersection of engineering and intelligence.

IntelliWell is an independent petroleum engineering technology project exploring how accessible simulation, visualization and data analytics can improve the way engineers understand production systems.

Our platform brings together well-focused analytical experiences and dedicated artificial lift simulation through Production LiftLab. We aim to connect domain knowledge with transparent computational methods and future AI-enabled decision support.

OUR TECHNOLOGY DIRECTION

Domain expertise meets
data science.

We believe meaningful intelligence starts with engineering fundamentals, quality data and models that can be explained.

01

Engineering simulation

Physics-informed exploration of production behavior, operating conditions and artificial lift alternatives.

02

Data science & analytics

Production-data preparation, visualization, descriptive metrics, trend analysis and reproducible engineering workflows.

03

Machine learning

Research direction: forecasting, anomaly detection, performance classification and model evaluation against engineering baselines.

04

AI-assisted guidance

Research direction: interpretable recommendations that explain trade-offs, assumptions and reasons behind engineering decisions.

Technology transparency

Simulation and analytics are the current focus. ML prediction, automated optimization and AI advisory features are development objectives; this website does not represent them as validated or production-ready systems.
